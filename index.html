LinkedIn Positioning Review

Prepared for Nottingham High Rollers

A clearer profile direction for a community that is bigger than a gaming club. The opportunity is to make the mission, local identity and emerging NHR universe unmistakable at first glance.

A place at the table.

That is the strongest positioning idea already present in the profile. The profile should make that promise visible before a visitor has to read deeply.

01 / What I found

The strongest asset is already there.

The profile has a clear mission, a defined Nottingham footprint, a January 2027 launch point and an unusually broad creative ecosystem. The main issue is hierarchy, not lack of substance.

01

Lead with the community

“There is a place at the table” is more memorable than a generic founder label. It gives the brand a human reason to exist.

02

Make Nottingham visible

The local identity is strategically important. It connects the profile to venues, schools, universities, retailers and community partners.

03

Sequence the universe

The original games and fictional properties are valuable, but they should support the community story rather than compete with it on first contact.

02 / Current positioning

What the profile currently communicates

The profile communicates a serious founder-led tabletop initiative with community, creative and long-term destination ambitions.

Already clear

Substance

The visitor can understand the breadth of NHR: wargaming, RPGs, board games, TCGs, painting, model building, campaigns, events and original IP.

Needs faster clarity

Hierarchy

The visitor has to work through a lot of information to understand the simplest proposition: who NHR is for, what makes it welcoming and why Nottingham matters.

03 / Headline

Turn the founder label into a positioning statement.

Current headline: Founder at Nottingham High Rollers. It is accurate, but it leaves the mission, audience and Nottingham community opportunity unstated.

Current

Founder at Nottingham High Rollers

Accurate and clean. The visitor still needs to open the About section to understand what NHR actually is.

Stronger direction

Founder + community + place

The strongest version makes the profile useful to players and to the local partners the profile explicitly wants to build relationships with.

OPTION 01

Founder, Nottingham High Rollers | Building a Tabletop Gaming & Hobby Community in Nottingham

Makes the founder role and the community mission immediately clear.

OPTION 02

Founder at Nottingham High Rollers | Tabletop Gaming, Hobby & Community | Nottingham

The clearest, most searchable version of the core positioning.

OPTION 03

Building Nottingham’s Tabletop Gaming Community | Founder, Nottingham High Rollers

Leads with the outcome and gives the profile a stronger local identity.

OPTION 04

Founder, Nottingham High Rollers | Making Tabletop Gaming More Welcoming in Nottingham

Turns the inclusive principle into a distinctive positioning statement.

OPTION 05

Nottingham High Rollers | Tabletop Gaming, Original Games & Community | Founder

Balances the community offer with the emerging original-game ecosystem.

04 / About

Keep the substance. Improve the journey.

The current About is rich and specific. Its biggest opportunity is to move from an organisation description into a clear first-person founder narrative with a stronger opening, simpler sequencing and a clearer invitation to connect.

Current About
Nottingham High Rollers is a Nottingham-based tabletop gaming and hobby community focused on bringing people together through gaming, creativity, learning and shared stories. Our activities span miniature wargaming, roleplaying games, board games, trading card games, miniature painting, model building, terrain, campaigns, community events and Nottingham High Rollers’ own original games and fictional universe. We are building NHR around a simple principle: There is a place at the table. Beginners, returning players and experienced hobbyists are all welcome. Nobody needs years of experience, an expensive collection or encyclopaedic rules knowledge to take part. Our public programme begins in January 2027, with regular gaming and hobby activities across Nottingham. Over time, the programme is intended to expand into beginner sessions, campaigns, tournaments, family activities, community projects, education, volunteering and partnerships with local organisations. Nottingham High Rollers is also developing its own connected creative ecosystem, including: • Nottingham High Rollers Trading Card Game • Vorta’s Arena • The High Rollers Chronicle • Trail of the High Rollers • Nottingham High Rollers Challenge Coin System • The wider NHR fictional universe Our longer-term ambition is to establish a permanent Nottingham High Rollers Hall in Nottingham: a community-focused destination combining tabletop gaming, hobby, events, creative facilities, retail, hospitality and local partnerships. We are interested in building constructive relationships with: • Nottingham venues and community organisations • Universities, colleges and schools • Gaming manufacturers and retailers • Hobby and creative businesses • Hospitality partners • Local suppliers • Community and volunteering organisations • Cultural and entertainment partners Community first. Fair play always. Nobody learns alone. Roll Dice • Tell Stories • Build Legends.
New About
I’m building Nottingham High Rollers around a simple idea: there should be a place at the table for everyone. NHR is a Nottingham-based tabletop gaming and hobby community bringing together miniature wargaming, roleplaying games, board games, trading card games, miniature painting, model building, campaigns and community events. The goal isn’t to make the hobby feel more complicated. It’s to make it easier to enter, easier to enjoy and easier to share. Beginners, returning players and experienced hobbyists should all have a way in, without needing years of experience, an expensive collection or encyclopaedic rules knowledge. Our public programme begins in January 2027, with regular gaming and hobby activities across Nottingham. From there, we’re building toward beginner sessions, campaigns, tournaments, family activities, community projects, education, volunteering and local partnerships. Alongside the community, I’m developing an original NHR creative universe, including the Nottingham High Rollers Trading Card Game, Vorta’s Arena, The High Rollers Chronicle, Trail of the High Rollers and the NHR Challenge Coin System. Longer term, the ambition is a permanent Nottingham High Rollers Hall: a community-focused destination bringing together tabletop gaming, hobby, events, creative facilities, retail, hospitality and local partnerships. If you’re a Nottingham venue, school, university, retailer, manufacturer, creative business, hospitality partner or community organisation interested in building something around tabletop gaming, I’d love to connect. Community first. Fair play always. Nobody learns alone. Roll Dice • Tell Stories • Build Legends.

06 / Banner

Give the mission a visual home.

No current banner asset was supplied with the profile material, so the recommendation focuses on the positioning gap rather than making claims about the existing artwork.

New banner direction

“A place at the table.”

The banner should make NHR feel welcoming, local and substantial in one glance. It should communicate tabletop gaming and community first, with the original universe kept as a secondary layer.

Job: Clarify the community proposition.

Visual language: Editorial fantasy, real hobby objects, warm light, restrained premium colour.

Safe area: Keep the left side visually quiet for the LinkedIn profile photo.

Status: Your new banner is attached separately.
